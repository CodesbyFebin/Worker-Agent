An AI worker agent in healthcare is an autonomous software system that can perform administrative tasks such as appointment scheduling, billing, and patient record management. These agents integrate with existing healthcare IT infrastructure (EHRs, practice management systems) and use large language models to process natural language instructions from clinical staff.
